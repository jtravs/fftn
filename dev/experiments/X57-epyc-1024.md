# X57: the EPYC's last exit-check cases: 120 and 72, and a radix choice that sees the batch

- **Status:** done (A: code placement; B adopted; C rejected; amendment 2: B's in-place limit
  capped at half the L3, 16 MiB)
- **Owner:** x57 session, 2026-11-18
- **Phase / task:** 1 (phase 1 exit check, the tail rule); follows X47, X54

## Question

The phase 1 exit check at 71307e0 still fails on the EPYC 7763 by two cases (run
20261118-194340-02a4, band ±1.00 %: 11 of 193 c2c cases beyond noise against FFTW, at most 9
allowed): 64 × 2 backward 1.28, 1024 × 3 1.22, 1024 1.21, 1024 backward 1.18, 64 × 16 1.14,
1024 × 16 1.12, 1018 1.10, 2048 1.10, 120 1.08, 1024 × 256 1.08, 100 1.07.

(A) Are 120 and 72, which moved 1.154 → 1.221 and 1.162 → 1.250 ns per point across
separate runs since main 8c18dd2, a real regression, and from what? (B) Can the radix choice
for 1024 see the plan's data, so that 16 · 64 (X54: 0.84–0.95 of the planner's time at batches
1–256, 1.25–1.27 at 1024 × 1024 and 1.14 on the 1024² plane) is taken only where it wins?
And can the same mechanism help 2048 and 64 × 16?

## Hypothesis

(B) The two-pass plan's first pass reads 16 streams at once. While the rows and their output
stay in the L3 that is cheap; when they do not, every stream misses at once and the three-pass
plan, whose first pass reads 4, wins (X54's counters: IPC 1.66 against 2.52 at 1024 × 1024,
as FFTW's own two-pass plan). So the choice depends on the data the plan touches, not the
row: a threshold on rows + output bytes in terms of the L3 of the core's complex (CCX).

## Change under test

Arm **B** (commit 2106e44 on base 71307e0): `target_profile::fit_overrides` lists lengths
whose radices depend on the data (`avx2` double: 1024 = 16 · 64, 768 = 12 · 64, its existing
override); `chain_builder::use_row_fit`, set by `plan_core.cpp` for c2c plans of one axis,
contiguous on both sides; `machine_profile::fit_row_bytes` is the most bytes of rows plus
output (rows alone in place) for which the two-pass radices are taken; beyond it the costs'
sequence (4 · 16 · 16, 4 · 12 · 16). `set_grid_sizes` sets it to three quarters of the L3 where
a private L2 has a large L3 behind it on x86-64 (`column_team_bytes`' key, the EPYC class). No
other target has a list, so no other target's plan changes. Rows of planes and volumes keep
the rules: this is a choice for one-axis plans only.

`FIT_ROW_BYTES=0` (fbench) turns the field off in the same binary: arm **B0** is B's commit
with the old plans, so B ÷ B0 is the plan change alone, with no code placement in it.

## Method

- Screens (not deciding; DMOG, Slurm, not `--serial`): forced radices against the planner on
  catalogue `x57` (below).
- Judging, EPYC 7763 (DMOG, GCC 16.2, `avx2`, one thread), `fbench_remote.py submit --serial`,
  interleaved process rounds, A/A control:
  1. standard catalogue, filter `^c2c-[0-9]+-b`, tier `full` (the exit check's 201 cases),
     arms B0, B, FFTW 3.3.11 (MEASURE);
  2. catalogue `x57`, all its cases of 768 and 1024, arms B0, B, FFTW;
  3. tracking tier, arms H = 71307e0 and B.
- Metric: fbench's median paired ratio B ÷ B0 (B ÷ H in 3) and its interval.
- **The moved cases** are those whose plan note differs between B and B0 in the run. By the
  mechanism they can only be one-axis c2c rows of 768 and 1024 points; every other case runs
  the same plan in the same binary and does not decide (AGENTS.md section 6).
- **Decision rule, written before any judging run:**
  - (a) Over the moved cases of run 1 (expected: 1024 × 1, 3, 16 and 256 out of place, 1024
    backward, 1024 × 1 and × 3 in place), the geometric mean of B ÷ B0 is at most 0.95 and its
    interval lies below 1 − band.
  - (b) No moved case of runs 1 and 2 is slower than 1 + band (a case slower than that is
    re-run once, `--serial`, and the re-run decides; if it stands, the threshold moves below it
    or the length leaves the list before adoption).
  - (c) No plane, volume or other length moves: the notes of B and B0 agree on every case
    outside 768 and 1024 rows in runs 1 and 3.
  - (d) Tracking tier, B ÷ H: geometric mean at most 1 + band; no case past 1 + band + 2 %
    unless the case's plan is unchanged and the objects its kernels live in are byte-identical
    (placement, which does not decide).
  - Xeon and Mac are not run: their profiles list no fit overrides, so their plans cannot move.

The first attempt at run 1 (20261118-210918-f4c4) is void: the backend refused
`FIT_ROW_BYTES=0`. Commit 9544f3d lets it through and is the commit runs 1 and 2 build
(the library's code is 2106e44's, reformatted in bd0dbd0); run 3 builds 9a2da0b, the same code.

*Amendment 1, written after the alignment screen below and before any judging run of arm C.*
Arm **C** (on B): GCC on x86-64 compiles the `avx2` units with `-falign-functions=64`, so that
a pass's distance from a 64-byte line no longer depends on what was linked before it. Every
function of those units moves, so every case running `avx2` code can move and all of them
count. `avx512` plans that call `avx2` leaves move too; NEON and Clang builds do not change.
Rule for C ÷ B, judged by `--serial` runs of the exit check's 201 cases and the tracking tier
on the EPYC, and the tracking tier on the Xeon:

- (a) EPYC: the geometric mean over the 201 cases, and over the tracking tier, is below 1
  (interval's upper end below 1); 72 and 120 are each faster than the band.
- (b) EPYC: no case slower than 1 + band + 2 %; a case past it is re-run once and the re-run
  decides.
- (c) Xeon: tracking tier's geometric mean at most 1 + band, no case past 1 + band + 2 %.
- (d) The library's text growth is reported; it does not decide (speed over size).

C fails if any of these fails, and is then not merged; B stands or falls by its own rule.

*Amendment 2 (2026-11-24, x57l session), written before any run of it: B's in-place limit.*
In place, `fit_row_bytes` (24 MiB on the EPYC 7763, three quarters of the CCX's 32 MiB L3)
counts the rows alone, so B's two-pass rows reach 1536 rows of 1024 points (24 MiB) and 2048 of
768, where runs 1 and 2 measured in place only to 1024 rows of 1024 (16 MiB, 0.928 of the
three-pass time in the screen). The maintainer (2026-11-24): measure the extrapolated part; if
it loses, or until measured, cap it at the footprint measured.

- **Plans** (from the code: `plan_core.cpp` gives `use_row_fit` the rows' bytes in place,
  `rules.cpp`'s `fitting_radices` the override or rule 4's sequence). 1024 points in place,
  forward and backward: 1024 rows (16 MiB) 16 · 64 in both arms; 1100, 1280 and 1536 rows
  (17.2–24 MiB) 16 · 64 in A, 4 · 16 · 16 in B; 1600 and 2048 rows (25–32 MiB) 4 · 16 · 16 in
  both. Out of place every batch from 1024 rows on is 32 MiB or more of rows and output:
  4 · 16 · 16 in both. 768 points in place: 1536 and 2048 rows (18 and 24 MiB) 12 · 64 in A,
  4 · 12 · 16 in B. The run's plan notes are the record; a case whose notes agree between the
  arms is not a moved case whatever this list says.
- **Arms** (one binary, `--serial`, EPYC 7763 on DMOG, GCC 16.2, `avx2`, one thread): **B** =
  `main` d4b169f with `FIT_ROW_BYTES=16777216` (16 MiB: in place the limit at 1024 rows of 1024
  points, the old plans beyond it; out of place it also drops 513–768 rows, which no case here
  has), listed first, so fbench's A/A control is B against itself; **A** = `main` d4b169f as
  it is (the two-pass rows to 24 MiB in place). The same binary, so A ÷ B is the plan change
  alone.
- **Cases**, catalogue `x57l`: the moved cases are 1024 × 1100, 1280, 1536 and 768 × 1536,
  2048, in place, forward and backward (10 cases; the 768 ones because the limit is one field
  in bytes and moves them too). Framing cases with one plan in both arms (1024 × 1024, 1600,
  2048 in place, both directions; 1024 × 1024–2048 out of place) do not decide.
- **Metric**: fbench's median paired ratio A ÷ B with its interval; the band is the A/A
  control's.
- **Decision rule.** Keep the limit at 24 MiB in place if, over the moved cases, (a) the
  geometric mean of A ÷ B is at most 1 and (b) no case is slower than 1 + band. A criterion
  missed by less than the band gets one `--serial` re-run of the same arms and cases, and the
  re-run decides (AGENTS.md section 6). Otherwise the limit is capped: in place at the largest
  measured footprint F (17.2, 18, 20, 24 MiB) such that the moved cases of at most F bytes
  pass (a) and (b) among themselves, or at 16 MiB (1024 rows of 1024, as measured in runs 1
  and 2) if none does; out of place the limit stays as it is.
- The Xeon and the Mac are not run: their profiles have no `fit_row_bytes`.
